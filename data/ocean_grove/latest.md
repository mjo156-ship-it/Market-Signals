## Assumptions & methodology

- **Depreciation basis split:** 40% land / 60% building · **100% bonus depreciation** taken in the purchase year on **20%** of the building basis (the cost-segregated share); the rest is depreciated straight-line over 27.5 years. Land is not depreciable.
- **Marginal tax rate:** 37.0% — STR losses assumed non-passive (avg stay <=7d + material participation). The year-1 depreciation loss is assumed usable against ordinary income; the resulting tax reduction is treated as a cash benefit.
- **Financing:** 25% down, 30-yr fixed @ 6.5%, 3% closing costs.
- **Operating costs:** management 15.0%, platform 3.0%, cleaning-net 4.0%, supplies 3.0%, capex reserve 5.0% (all % of gross); insurance 0.6% & maintenance 1.0% of price; utilities $4,800/yr; STR license $750/yr.
- **Seasonal revenue model:** peak 11wk @ 90% occ, shoulder 8wk @ 60% (55% of peak rate), off 33wk @ 22% (32% of peak rate). Peak weekly rate by bedroom count.
- **Break-even definition:** Three tests are reported. purchase_year = year-1 after-tax operating cash flow (financed) >= $0, i.e. break-even net of tax WITH bonus depreciation on the cost-segregated share of the building. sustained = stabilized (yr 2+, straight-line depreciation only) after-tax cash flow (financed) >= $0 (the strict self-funding test). operating_allcash = stabilized after-tax cash flow >= $0 unlevered. break_even_down_pct = equity fraction needed to self-fund annually; horizon_years = years the one-time yr-1 tax windfall keeps the cumulative position non-negative.

### Ocean Grove notes

- Most Ocean Grove lots sit on a **99-year ground lease from the Ocean Grove Camp Meeting Association (CMA)** — the buyer typically owns the *building* on leased land. An annual ground-lease fee is included in operating costs where applicable. Because the land is not owned, a buyer could arguably justify a higher building allocation than the 40/60 split assumed here (which would *increase* the year-1 depreciation shield).
- **Neptune Township has been tightening STR rules** (minimum-stay and bonding requirements). Confirm current licensing/minimum-stay before underwriting; these can materially change occupancy and cost assumptions.
- Summer bookings are typically **Saturday-to-Saturday weekly**, reflected in the weekly-rate seasonal model above.
